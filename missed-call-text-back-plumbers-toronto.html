Toronto plumbing calls

When Toronto plumbing calls get missed

Plumbing calls are short-fuse: a leaking water heater, a backed-up drain or a running toilet. The caller who reaches voicemail usually calls the next listing within minutes.

Solo plumbers and small crews in Toronto spend much of the day under sinks and in crawl spaces, where answering the phone isn't possible.

Storm days bring a burst of flooded-basement and sump calls across older neighbourhoods. A text back keeps those callers from moving on while you're on another job.
