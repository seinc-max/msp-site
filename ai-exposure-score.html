Getting started

The 15 Diagnostic Questions

Answer each question below. Yes = 1 point. No = 0 points. Then calculate your score.

1. Does your business track exactly which public generative AI applications (ChatGPT, Claude, deep-seek) employees access on corporate networks?

2. Do you maintain an updated, legally binding AI Acceptable Use Policy signed by all staff elements?

3. Is sensitive client or corporate data structurally ring-fenced to prevent it from training public LLM models?

4. Have you configured Microsoft 365 Copilot (or Google Workspace AI) permissions to prevent employees from surfacing restricted corporate documents?

5. Have you mapped your employee workflows to verify that proprietary intellectual property isn't leaking into external vectors?

6. Do you have formal technical gatekeepers configured to monitor public AI prompt inputs for corporate privilege leaks?

7. Are 100% of your automated business workflows routed through sovereign architecture that prevents systemic availability dependencies or vendor-level lockouts?

8. Does your firm have technical controls to block unsanctioned AI browser extensions from reading on-screen data?

9. Are your primary department managers trained to detect and trace unverified, AI-generated deliverables before they reach clients?

10. Do employees receive documented, mandatory training specifically covering the risks of Shadow AI and data privacy?

11. Do your standard client agreements or service contracts explicitly state how your firm uses AI to process their confidential data?

12. Have you formally opted your corporate accounts out of third-party data training across major SaaS vendors (e.g., Zoom, Slack, Adobe)?

13. Do you require third-party vendors to sign addendums guaranteeing they will not use your company's data to train their AI models?

14. Does your incident response plan include specific protocols for tracing and mitigating an AI-related data leak?

15. Is your cyber insurance policy verified to cover data breaches or liability claims originating from employee AI misuse?
