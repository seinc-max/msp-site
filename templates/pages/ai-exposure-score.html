Getting started

15 questions

Answer each question below. Yes = 1 point. No = 0 points. Then calculate your score.

1. Do you know which public AI tools, such as ChatGPT or Claude, your people use on the firm's network?

2. Do you have a written AI use policy that your people have signed?

3. Is client and firm data kept out of public AI tools, so it is not used to train those models?

4. Have you set Microsoft 365 Copilot, or Google Workspace AI, so people cannot open files they should not see?

5. Have you checked whether client work, or your own know-how, is being pasted into outside AI tools?

6. Do you watch what people type into public AI tools, so a privileged note does not leave the firm?

7. Do your automations run in systems you control, so a vendor outage or lock-in can't stop client work?

8. Can you block unapproved AI browser add-ons from reading what is on screen?

9. Can your managers spot an AI-written draft before it goes to a client?

10. Have your people had training on the risks of using public AI with client files?

11. Do your client agreements say how the firm uses AI with their confidential information?

12. Have you turned off the setting that lets vendors such as Zoom, Slack, or Adobe train their AI on your firm's accounts?

13. Do your vendors agree in writing that they will not use your firm's data to train their AI?

14. If client data leaks through an AI tool, does your incident plan say how you trace it and stop it?

15. Does your cyber insurance cover a breach or a claim that starts with someone at the firm misusing AI?
