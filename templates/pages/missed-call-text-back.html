Missed calls can send money out the door

Calls go to voicemail

Nearly half of calls to home-service businesses never reach a person (Invoca, 2026). You're paying for leads that ring once and die in voicemail. The homeowner with a flooded basement, a dead furnace or no hot water has already called the next business by the time you look at your phone.

No time to call everyone back

You're under a sink, on a roof, or driving between jobs. The painter quoting a living room, the landscaper mid-install, the electrician in a panel with the power off — none of them can stop and dial back. By the time you're free, the customer has moved on.

Missed calls waste ad spend

Every paid lead that reaches voicemail wastes the ad spend that generated it — and that caller may choose another company instead. A garage-door repair, a pest-control emergency, a junk-removal cleanout: when they call, they need someone now.

Common questions

Is this an AI receptionist?

No. This texts your caller after a missed call. You still call them back and land the job. If you want a receptionist that answers nights, weekends, and after hours and captures job requests, see the AI receptionist.

Do I have to set this up myself?

No. We install it and run it. You're not buying software to configure yourself.

What happens after a missed call?

Your caller gets a text from your business number within minutes. They reply with what they need, and you get an alert.

What does the text say?

Whatever you approve. A common start: "Hi, this is Mike from Lakeside Heating. Sorry I missed your call. Reply here and I'll call you back the minute I'm free. Reply STOP to opt out." Every first text includes STOP to opt out.

Isn't voicemail enough?

Many callers hang up instead of leaving a voicemail and call the next name on Google. A text reaches them while their phone is still in their hand, so the conversation starts before they call someone else.

Can I keep my existing number?

Yes. You keep your existing number — we set up call forwarding, no porting. We confirm the forwarding details on the call.

Why is there a setup fee?

The $199 covers the install: connecting your number, approving the wording and going live. It's not a random surcharge.

No. We work directly with business owners like you. We don't resell under other brands, and we don't replace all your software.
