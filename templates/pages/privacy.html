What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal information Trueline IT collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers this website, our inquiry forms, calls and emails with us, and calls answered by our AI Receptionist for the businesses that use it.

What we collect and why

Inquiry forms

Our professional-services pages, guides and calculators include a short inquiry form that asks for your name and email address. That covers AI governance pages, the AI Liability Assessment, Fractional AI Officer, blog posts, and the ROI, exposure and margin tools.

When you submit the form, your name and email are sent to HubSpot, our customer relationship management provider. We use them to reply to your inquiry and to follow up about our services. By submitting the form, you consent to this collection and use.

Calls and emails

If you call or email us, we keep only the details you share, such as your name, phone number, and email. We use them to respond and to follow up about our services.

Website tools

We use these third-party tools, which may collect data about site visitors:

  • Google Analytics & Google Tag Manager: collects anonymized usage data to help us understand how visitors interact with our site.
  • Clarity: session recording of clicks, scrolls and mouse movement.
  • HubSpot: stores contact information submitted through inquiry forms on our professional-services pages, guides and calculators.
  • Google Ads: serves ads and tracks conversions via cookies.

AI Receptionist calls

If a business that uses our AI Receptionist has call recording turned on, the AI tells callers at the start of the call. We keep those call recordings and transcripts, using outside service providers, only to run that business's service. We don't sell them or use them to train public AI models. If the business cancels, we delete them 30 days later, except what the law requires us to keep. Questions about a recording or transcript? Email hello@truelineit.com.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to reply to your inquiries, improve our website, and contact you about our services. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Who handles it for us

The website tools listed above (Google, Clarity and HubSpot) handle some of this information for us. AI Receptionist recordings and transcripts are kept using outside service providers, only to run that business's service.

Cookies

Our site uses cookies for analytics and advertising. You can turn cookies off in your browser settings, but some parts of the site may not work as well.

How long we keep it

We keep contact information for as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy, or as long as the law requires. For AI Receptionist recordings and transcripts, see AI Receptionist calls.

Your rights

If you are a resident of Canada, you have rights under PIPEDA, Canada's federal private-sector privacy law. They include the right to see (access), correct, or ask us to delete your personal information. To use these rights, email hello@truelineit.com.
