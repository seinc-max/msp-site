Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Trueline IT ("we", "us") collects, why, and how you can see, correct or ask us to delete it. It covers this website, our inquiry forms, calls, texts and emails with us, and calls answered by our AI Receptionist for the businesses that use it. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

1. What we collect and why

Inquiry forms

Some of our pages, guides and calculators have a short inquiry form that asks for your name and email address. These include AI governance pages, the AI Liability Assessment, Fractional AI Officer, blog posts, and the ROI, exposure and margin tools.

When you submit the form, your name and email are sent to HubSpot, the customer relationship management (CRM) tool we use to manage inquiries. We use them to reply to your inquiry and to follow up about our services. By submitting the form, you consent to this collection and use.

Calls, texts and emails with us

If you call, text or email us, we keep the details you share, such as your name, phone number, email and messages. We use them to reply and to follow up about our services. Reply STOP to any text and we stop texting you.

If you call or text a business that uses our services

We use the caller's phone number, messages, and any call recordings and transcripts only to run that business's service. If the business has call recording on, the AI tells callers at the start of the call. We don't sell any of it or use it to train public AI models.

Website visits

When you visit our site, the tools in the next section may collect data about your visit.

3. Cookies and session recording

Our site uses cookies for analytics and advertising, and Microsoft Clarity records how you click and scroll (session recording). A small banner at the bottom of the page lets you accept or decline. The tools stay on unless you decline.

If you decline, Google's analytics and ad tools stop using cookies, and session recording and other analytics stop. Your browser remembers your choice.

Other ways to opt out:

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the site may not work as well.
  • Turn off personalized Google ads at Google My Ad Center.
  • Stop Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

4. How long we keep information

  • Inquiry form information (name, email): 24 months after our last contact with you.
  • Client customer data: while the client is paying, plus 30 days after they cancel.
  • AI Receptionist recordings and transcripts: 30 days after the client cancels.
  • Google Analytics data: 14 months.
  • Microsoft Clarity session recordings: 30 days.
  • Payment records: 7 years.
  • Card numbers: we never store them.

We keep other contact details and messages only as long as we need them for the reasons in this policy, or as long as the law requires.

5. Your rights, questions and complaints

Under PIPEDA, Canada's federal private-sector privacy law, you can see (access) your personal information, correct it, and ask us to delete it. To do any of these, email hello@truelineit.com. We may ask you to confirm who you are first. We'll reply within 30 days. If we need more time, or can't do what you ask (for example, because the law requires us to keep something), we'll tell you why in writing.

Our privacy contact is responsible for this policy and can be reached at the same email or at the address below. Please contact us first so we can try to fix a concern. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
