Calls, texts, and AI

A few plain facts about how our services work:

  • Our AI may answer your calls. When someone calls you, they may be talking to our AI receptionist, not a person. We tell callers when that's happening.
  • Calls may be recorded. If recording is on, our AI tells callers at the start of the call. Recordings and transcripts are used only to run your service. Some places require every person on a call to agree to recording; you're responsible for meeting the rules where you and your callers are. See our Privacy Policy at truelineit.com/privacy for how we handle personal information.
  • Missed-call texts go out about 2 minutes after a missed call. That timing is how the service is designed to work. It's our aim, not a promise that every single text lands within that window. If you reply STOP to our texts, we stop — always.
  • Texts and emails follow Canada's anti-spam law. We only send messages to people who have agreed to hear from you or have an existing business relationship with you. Every text says it's from your business and tells people how to stop: reply STOP. Every email has an unsubscribe link. We act on STOP or unsubscribe, and always within 10 business days.
  • You need the right to contact your customers. For anything where we text or call your customers for you (reviews, reminders, follow-ups), it's your job to make sure you have their consent. If you give us a bad list, that's on you. We send in good faith for you.
  • Text and call costs are your carrier's. Your phone plan's normal message and data rates apply.
  • AI can be wrong. Our AI answers from your materials and instructions. For anything sensitive, like legal, medical or money questions, we recommend a human review. We'll do our best, but we can't promise the AI is always right.
