4. Calls, texts and AI

  • AI calls. When someone calls you, they may be talking to our AI receptionist, not a person. Callers are told they're speaking with an AI assistant.
  • Recording. If call recording is on, our AI tells callers at the start of the call. Recordings and transcripts are used only to run your service. Some places require every person on a call to agree to recording; you're responsible for meeting the rules where you and your callers are.
  • Missed-call texts. They're designed to reach your caller within minutes. That's our aim, not a promise that every single text arrives in that time.
  • Canada's anti-spam law (CASL). We text only people who agreed to hear from you or have an existing business relationship with you. Cold Email Outreach goes only to published business email addresses with no note saying they don't want messages, and only about the person's work. Every message names your business, every email gives your mailing address, and each one has a way to stop: reply STOP to a text, or click unsubscribe in an email. We act on it, and always within 10 business days.
  • Your contact list. You're responsible for having consent to contact the customers on the list you give us, and for making sure what we send for you meets CASL.
  • Reviews. We ask your customers for reviews. We never write, buy, post or filter reviews.
  • AI can be wrong. Our AI answers from your materials and instructions. For anything sensitive, like legal, medical or money questions, we recommend a human review.
