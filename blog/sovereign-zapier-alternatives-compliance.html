The Hidden Financial and Security Penalties of Public Cloud Tools

If you manage a growing business, you already know how fast automation can scale your daily operations. Connecting your favorite software tools lets data move automatically between your customer database, your email systems, and your financial ledgers. But relying on traditional public cloud tools introduces two major problems that hit your bottom line:

  • The "Task Tax" Inflation: The more your business grows, the more automated tasks you run, causing your software bills to skyrocket unpredictably every month. You are essentially penalized for scaling your own company.
