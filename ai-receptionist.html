Questions

Straight answers

Is this the same as Missed-Call Text-Back?

No. Missed-call text-back, for trades and contractors, is a text after you miss a call: $149 a month plus a $199 setup. The AI receptionist answers after hours and on weekends, captures job requests and sends urgent calls to you: $299 a month plus a one-time $299 setup.

Is this DIY software?

No. You don't configure prompts or call flows. If that's what you want, this isn't the right fit.

Is there human backup, like a traditional answering service?

It's AI voice, with urgent calls going to you. It is not a live human receptionist desk.

Do your callers know they're speaking with AI?

Yes. They're told they're speaking with an AI assistant.

Why a setup fee when some apps say $0 setup?

The $299 setup is the hands-on install: training on your services, hours, and the pricing questions callers ask, so you aren't left wiring it alone.

Yes. Play the sample call on this page, or call for a walk-through of how it answers your calls.
