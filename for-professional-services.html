You don't have to call yourself a "professional services firm." If client work sits on your computers and your people are already using AI to move faster, you're in the right place.

The file leaves the building at the worst time. Here's where you'd lose it.

  1. Law firm, the night before a filing. Your associate pastes a client memo into a public tool to tighten the wording. The memo is privileged. You find out when a client asks where your AI policy is.
  2. Accountant at month-end. A staff accountant drops a client's statements into ChatGPT to "clean the labels." The numbers are now on a public tool. You still have to sign the return.
  3. Financial advisor before a review meeting. Copilot answers a question about a client's portfolio from a folder the whole office can open. The junior who asked didn't need that file.
  4. Insurance agency on a renewal. A claim note goes into a free AI chat so the summary is faster. Your carrier's renewal form asks which AI tools your team uses. You don't have a written answer.
  5. Architecture firm the night a deadline moves. Drawings and a client brief go into a public tool to draft the email. You wanted the hour back. You didn't want the brief in someone else's model.
