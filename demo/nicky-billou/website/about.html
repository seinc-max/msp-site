ABOUT NICKY

Greatness isn’t given. It’s claimed.

Nicky Billou was born in Tehran, Iran, and left with his family. He has lived in five countries on three continents. He graduated Magna Cum Laude from Georgetown University — and he has read more than 3,000 books.

He is the co-founder of eCircle Academy, and the creator of the Thought Leader / Heart Leader™ Designation. His mission is simple: help leaders build powerful, relevant personal brands — and become the Steve Jobs of their industry, through personal branding and podcast guesting.

On both sides of the microphone

Nicky has been a guest on more than 600 shows. He hosts The Thought Leader Revolution — over 830 episodes — and The Sovereign Man Podcast.

He has written four best-selling books. He speaks for companies like RBC, Lululemon, Royal LePage, and TorStar Media.

In July 2025, Raymond Aaron — New York Times bestselling author — introduced him at the Transformational Leadership Council, the group founded by Jack Canfield.
