You do the work in the chair, on the table or in the bay. We make sure the phone keeps booking.

Every missed call is an empty slot. Here's where you'd lose them.

  1. Saturday no-show at the salon. Your 2 PM doesn't show, and you're mid-colour on your 1:30. A new caller wants something today. Their request reaches you while there's still time to fill the chair.
  2. Barbershop at the lunch rush. Three of your chairs are full and two people are waiting when the phone rings for a fade tomorrow. You don't stop the clippers, and you still get the name, the time they want and their number.
  3. Pet groomer with a dog on the table. You can't let go of a wet doodle to answer a call. The client who wants to move Thursday's groom gets an answer, and you get the request to rebook.
  4. Auto detailer mid-polish. You're halfway through a paint correction when someone calls about an interior detail this week. You get the request with their car and the day they want, and you confirm when your hands are free.
  5. Physio clinic between patients. You're with someone on the table and the front desk line is ringing. A new client looking for a first visit gets an answer instead of calling the next clinic on the list.

Is this just for hair salons?

No. If you're a barber, nail studio, day spa, pet groomer, auto detailer, dental office, physio clinic, massage therapy clinic, tattoo studio or fitness studio and you book by phone, call (647) 360-5774 and tell us how your day runs.
