Toronto HVAC calls

When Toronto HVAC calls get missed

Toronto's cold snaps bring waves of no-heat calls in a few hours. Many older homes in East York, the Beaches and the Annex still run hot-water boilers and radiators, and those calls can't wait for Monday.

Summer heat warnings bring a surge of AC no-cool calls, often from condo residents with fan coils or heat pumps that need a technician who knows the building.

Heat pump and high-efficiency furnace replacement quotes run all year as homeowners look at rebates and financing.

Local programs, licensing and area codes

Home energy upgrades. The City of Toronto offers the Toronto Home Energy Loan Program (HELP) for upgrades such as heat pumps. Callers often ask about it; the receptionist can note the question for your callback.
Gas licensing. Gas appliance work in Ontario is regulated by the TSSA and done by licensed gas technicians. The receptionist never diagnoses; it takes details and passes them on.
Area codes. Calls come from 416, 647 and 437 in the city and 905 and 289 from the suburbs. It works on your existing number.
