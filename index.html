Common questions

You've probably got questions.

What size businesses do you work with?

We specialize in businesses with 5 to 50 employees who don't have an in-house IT person. If that's you, you're our ideal client.

What does "flat rate" actually mean?

You pay a fixed monthly fee per user. Doesn't matter how many support tickets you submit, how long calls take, or what we have to fix. Your bill is the same every month.

What if we already have some IT in place?

No problem. We do a full assessment during onboarding and work with what you have. If something needs to change, we'll tell you plainly and give you options.

How fast do you respond when something breaks?

Critical issues get same-day response. For routine requests, we aim to respond within a few hours during business hours. You'll always hear back from a real person.

Is there a contract?

Month-to-month. We earn your business every month. If you're not happy, you can cancel with 30 days notice — no penalties, no drama.
